Personal notes

A very organised work in progress

11 November 2026

I like a plan. Preferably one with headings, folders and enough notes for future me to retrace every step. To be completely honest, I have Excel files for my entire personal life. Oops!

This blog is my current project: a little corner of the internet where I’m organising my thoughts, one blog post at a time.

I have always written stuff, about me, about others. It has always been my way of processing things and understanding the world around me. I have a way with words, and they have a way with me.

These notes will be home to the rest of me: things I’m learning, unsolicited but nonetheless given opinions and thoughts that don’t quite belong in a figure legend.

I’ll document it as I go. Obviously. 🦄
