My first science note

11 November 2026

I spend a lot of time asking cells what they’re up to. They answer in RNA, which is a wonderfully inconvenient way to have a conversation.

I’m Olena-Maria, a computational biologist and PhD candidate exploring single-cell transcriptomics, long-read sequencing and planarian regeneration. This is where I’ll share the biological questions I’m chasing, the methods I’m figuring out, and the occasional plot that raises more questions than it answers.

Bring your curiosity. I’ll bring the excessively organised notes. 🦄
